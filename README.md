# Retail Store Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on retail store transaction data to identify purchasing patterns, customer demographics, payment preferences, and relationships between numerical variables.

The objective is to generate actionable insights that can help understand customer behavior and support data-driven sales strategies.

## 🎯 Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Understanding sales distribution across product categories
- Analyzing discounts applied to previous purchases
- Comparing sales across different payment methods
- Understanding purchasing behavior by gender
- Identifying the most frequently purchased items
- Analyzing average customer ratings across age groups
- Studying correlations between numerical variables

## 🔍 Key Questions

1. What is the total amount generated by each product category?
2. How are discounts related to previous purchases?
3. Which payment methods contribute to the total sales amount?
4. How does total spending vary by gender?
5. What are the top 10 most purchased items?
6. How does the average rating vary across different age groups?
7. What relationships exist between the numerical variables?

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Total Amount by Category

Analyzed total sales generated by different product categories to identify categories contributing most to overall revenue.

### 2. Discount vs Previous Purchases

Examined the relationship between discounts and customers' previous purchase behavior to identify purchasing patterns.

### 3. Amount by Payment Method

Compared total transaction amounts across different payment methods to understand customer payment preferences.

### 4. Total Amount by Gender

Analyzed spending patterns across customer genders to identify differences in total purchasing amounts.

### 5. Top 10 Items Purchased

Identified the ten most frequently purchased products to understand high-demand items.

### 6. Average Rating by Age Group

Grouped customers into age groups and calculated average ratings to understand differences in customer feedback across demographics.

### 7. Correlation Analysis

Calculated correlations between numerical variables and visualized them using a correlation heatmap to identify potential relationships between variables.
